About

I am an architect and urban designer, trained across three scales. I earned a Bachelor of Engineering in Architecture at the City University of Wuhan, then a Master of Architecture and a Master of Urban Design at Washington University in St. Louis. I am now applying to PhD programs in urban planning, for Fall 2027, to work at the scale where the structure of a whole neighborhood decides who reaches care.

Practice

At Christner Architects I worked on healthcare projects, including the MU Children's Hospital surgery suite and the Missouri Baptist Medical Center linear accelerator addition. Earlier, at hellmuth + bicknese, I worked on public-facing community buildings such as the Saint Louis Zoo Education Center.

Research and teaching

I ran early-stage fieldwork for a citywide aging-care facility plan in Xiangyang, China, collecting household data on family composition, health, and care needs. I was a research assistant on the SMOOTH House, a net-zero Solar Decathlon entry, under Dr. Hongxi Yin, and I taught as a teaching assistant at WashU in environmental and building systems.

Writing

My abstract “Proximity is not access: NYC and Shanghai” was accepted to the Urban Transitions Conference in 2026, and I wrote a paper for the Applied and Computational Engineering Conference in 2023.

Recognition

  • Featured in Dezeen, Washington University in St. Louis school show, 2024.
  • Solar Decathlon Build Challenge, competition winner, for the SMOOTH occupational-therapy home.
  • China Green Building Council National Green Building Design Skills Competition, Outstanding Award, 2020.
  • Hubei Institute of Planning and Design, Excellence in Design Award (third prize), for a Xiangyang senior-living master plan, 2019.

Now

I am in Richmond, Virginia, where my time at the Ronald McDonald House has shown me the social support that hospitals hand to others: housing for families of hospitalized children, and neighbors who cook and keep company.
