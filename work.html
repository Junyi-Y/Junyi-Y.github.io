Making

Eight projects in two modes: spatial research that tests how neighborhoods shape who reaches care, and design and practice across the building and city scale. Together they build to one question, whether the space around care lets people actually reach it.

Spatial research

  • Aging Care Facility Planning, Xiangyang Xiangyang, China · 2018 · research

  • Plug In The Canal Bangkok · 2023 · field research A Bangkok field study measuring street-level heat and recording who could actually use a shaded space, and who could not. Proximity is not access, tested on the ground.
  • St. Louis Spatial Equity Mapping Washington University · Spring 2024 · GIS GIS mapping of how extreme-heat vulnerability concentrates in St. Louis, and how proximity to clinics and cooling fails to become real access. The precursor to my question of for whom access matters most.
  • Aging Care Facility Planning, Xiangyang Xiangyang, China · 2018 · research A citywide, three-tier aging-care plan for Xiangyang, built from household surveys of the city's older residents. The survey's core finding, that family living nearby does not guarantee daily support, is the seed of my dissertation question.
  • MU Children's Hospital Surgery Suite Christner Architects · 2024 · practice Healthcare programming and circulation on a children's surgery suite. This is where my question began, when a corridor meant for families to rest was cut so beds could move faster.
  • Making Places of Joy Atlanta · 2024 · urban design A 30-year plan for South Atlanta that turns sites once marked by injustice into a connected system of cultural and community places.
  • One Planet Living Community Chicago · 2023 · urban design A neighborhood master plan as a system for resilience.
  • International Students Housing St. Louis · 2023 · M.Arch Movable panels against post-pandemic isolation.
  • SMOOTH House Solar Decathlon · 2022 · research assistant SMOOTH, a net-zero smart home for occupational therapy and a Solar Decathlon winner, built around a healing garden.
