Reading note

Age-friendly communities

Andy Hong, HARP Lab, University of Utah · reading note

The argument. Hong's Healthy Aging and Resilient Places Lab frames healthy aging as a property of place, not only of individuals. Working through age-friendly cities and communities, and through neighborhood-scale data, it studies how the built environment shapes the social and environmental determinants of health as people age.

For my research. This is my anchor. Hong treats aging and the built environment as a single question, which is the ground my work stands on. Where I extend him is the scale below the neighborhood: his community-level data is strong on the neighborhood but stops short of the individual facility and the streets immediately around it, and that is where I argue access is won or lost.

Source · Age-friendly community interventions: a scoping review (2023) · HARP Lab
