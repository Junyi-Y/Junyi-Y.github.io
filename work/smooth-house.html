A net-zero smart home for occupational therapy, built around a healing garden.

SMOOTH House (SMart hOme for Occupational THerapy), a Solar Decathlon Build Challenge entry and competition winner · research assistant under Dr. Hongxi Yin, 2022.

SMOOTH, a smart home for occupational therapy, is a net-zero house built for the Solar Decathlon Build Challenge and a competition winner. As a research assistant under Dr. Hongxi Yin, I produced plan and section drawings, construction details, and renderings.

The approach

The house pairs occupational-therapy healing with net-zero performance: it is built largely of engineered bamboo, with a geothermal system, rooftop solar, and daylight brought deep into the plan. At its center is a healing garden, a restorative space built into the plan rather than added to it.

The design

From the plan and the bamboo wall assembly to the garden at the center.

In my research

Giving rest and recovery their own place, rather than treating them as leftover, is the building-scale version of the question I now ask at the neighborhood scale.
