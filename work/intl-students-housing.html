Movable panels against post-pandemic isolation.

Washington University in St. Louis, M.Arch, with Sungho Kim · St. Louis · 2023 · individual work.

An international student residence built around movable panels that reconfigure boundaries at several scales, from a wall to a window to a door. Designed in the post-pandemic moment, it lets residents move between privacy and shared space.

The approach

Extended isolation had shown how much daily life depends on contact. The design makes the boundary itself adjustable: the same common room can be set for unit-based sharing, adapted for privacy, or opened as a dormitory extension, so residents can choose contact or retreat.

The design

From the plan and section to the panel system and the street.

In my research

Isolation, and the spatial means to relieve it, is a thread that runs directly into my current work.
