Turning contested ground into a system of shared places.

Washington University in St. Louis, MUD program, with Prof. Patty Heyda · Atlanta · Spring 2024 · team and individual work.

Making Places of Joy is a thirty-year framework for South Atlanta, built on the neighborhood's own places of joy: the anchor institutions, gathering spaces, and parks that hold its identity, including sites once marked by injustice. The proposal connects them into a single system, with community voices guiding the work.

The approach

The plan works through connectivity and green infrastructure, affordable and rent-to-own housing, access to healthy food, and cultural and economic anchors, phased so that foundations come first and cultural destinations follow. It reads shared, everyday places as social infrastructure.
