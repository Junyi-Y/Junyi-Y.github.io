A neighborhood master plan as a system for resilience.

Washington University in St. Louis, MUD program, with John Hoal · Chicago · 2023 · individual work.

A neighborhood master plan for Chicago, organized around One Planet Living principles. Working individually in John Hoal's studio, I structured two districts and three neighborhoods as a single environmental system.

The approach

The plan treats a neighborhood as a system, where transport, water, ecology, and public space set the terms for who can live and move well. It is built around sustainable transport, water, and materials: a green-finger water-management system, wetland and woodland parks, bike and shuttle networks, and mixed building types that break and reconnect the river edge.

The design

From the neighborhood as a whole to the section at the water's edge.

In my research

That systems view of the neighborhood, where the structure of a place decides who can live well in it, is the scale my dissertation works at.
